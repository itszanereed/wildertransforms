Questions

FAQ

Do I need gym experience?

No. Whether you've never touched a barbell or you've been training for years, your plan is built around where you are right now.

Is this just a workout plan?

No. Training and nutrition are one pillar. We also work on mindset, discipline, confidence, and the parts of life that keep you stuck, and you get a brotherhood to grow with.

Is this therapy?

No. I'm a coach, not a licensed therapist. I'll be real with you, support you, and hold you accountable, and if something needs professional help, I'll encourage you to get it.

How much time does it take each week?

Plan for 3–5 training sessions, a weekly check-in form, and one call. Everything is built to fit your schedule.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the coaching tier that fits your goals. We'll go over everything on your call, with no pressure to commit.
